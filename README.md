# Transilience AI — Legal & Takedown Operations

> Official account of the Transilience AI Legal & Takedown team.

## About

[Transilience AI](https://www.transilience.ai) ([@transilienceai](https://github.com/transilienceai)) provides brand-protection and takedown services. This account is used by our Legal & Takedown team to submit and manage takedown requests **as an authorized agent acting on behalf of our clients**.

## Scope of Services

We act for our clients in matters including:

- **Copyright infringement** — notices under the DMCA (17 U.S.C. § 512) and equivalent laws in other jurisdictions
- **Trademark infringement and impersonation** — unauthorized use of client names, logos and brand identity
- **Phishing and fraud** — phishing kits, credential-harvesting pages and malicious repositories targeting our clients or their customers
- **Data exposure** — leaked proprietary source code, credentials and confidential information
- **Unauthorized distribution** — counterfeit or unlicensed copies of client software

## Authorization

Every request submitted from this account is made on behalf of, and with the authorization of, the rights holder concerned. Notices include the information required by applicable law, including a statement of good-faith belief that the reported use is not authorized and a statement, under penalty of perjury, that we are authorized to act on behalf of the rights holder.

## If You Have Received a Notice

1. Review the notice to identify the specific material and the rights holder concerned.
2. If you believe the material was reported in error, you may file a counter-notice through the platform's process — for GitHub, see the [guide to submitting a DMCA counter notice](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/guide-to-submitting-a-dmca-counter-notice).
3. You may also contact us directly to resolve the matter.
